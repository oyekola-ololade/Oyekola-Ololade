Commercial work

I work where automation becomes operational infrastructure.

The useful work is rarely “connect app A to app B.” It is understanding state, failure, data, permissions, model behavior and what the business is allowed to trust.

01 / AUDIT

Workflow & systems audits

Trace how state and data move through an existing automation and identify silent failure, brittle assumptions and false-success paths.

  • state lineage & provenance
  • failure-chain analysis
  • integration boundary review
02 / BUILD

AI automation builds

Design workflow-heavy AI systems with explicit control boundaries rather than relying on the model to behave correctly.

  • n8n orchestration
  • multi-agent workflows
  • human approval & handoff
03 / REPAIR

Repair & refactoring

Take a workflow that technically runs but cannot be trusted and rebuild the weak points without pretending the whole system needs replacing.

  • idempotency & retries
  • state reconciliation
  • error-path design
